Veypoint — Support

Instrument cluster for your vehicle, over Bluetooth.

Common questions

  • The app cannot find my car. Make sure Bluetooth is on and you are within a few meters of the vehicle. The in-app Diagnostics screen walks through each requirement and points at the one that is failing.
  • Pairing does not finish. Pairing requires a tap of your key card on the car's console reader. Follow the guided setup flow in Settings, and keep the card flat on the sensing area for a few seconds.
  • The speed shows without the car connected. That is the GPS fallback: when the Bluetooth link is down, Veypoint shows your phone's own speed so the dial stays useful.
  • Restoring a purchase. Open Settings → Membership → the paywall, and tap "Restore purchases" while signed in to the same Apple account.
