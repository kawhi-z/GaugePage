Gauge — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 31, 2026

Gauge is an instrument-cluster app that connects to your own vehicle over Bluetooth and shows live driving data — speed, battery, tire pressure and charging. It is built so that your data stays on your phone. Gauge has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs, and it operates no server of its own.

Data Gauge handles, and where it stays

  • Vehicle data. Speed, battery level, tire pressure and charging state are read directly from your vehicle over an encrypted Bluetooth link. They are shown on screen and processed only on your device. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else.
  • Location (GPS). With your permission, Gauge uses your phone's GPS to show your speed when the vehicle is out of Bluetooth range, and to keep the speedometer in step with the car while connected. Your location is used in the moment, on the device, and never leaves your phone. Gauge keeps no history of where you have been.
  • Vehicle identifiers and pairing keys. The vehicle identification number (VIN) you enter and the cryptographic key that pairs your phone with the car are stored only on your device. The private key never leaves it.
  • Diagnostic logs. Gauge keeps a local log of its Bluetooth communication to help diagnose connection problems. Logs stay on your device unless you explicitly choose to export and share them yourself.

Purchases

Gauge Pro subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Your entitlement status is verified by Apple's StoreKit framework and remembered on your device.

What Gauge does not do

  • No data is collected, sold or shared with third parties.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services are embedded.
  • No advertising identifiers are read or used.
  • No account is created, and no personal information is requested.

Data retention and deletion

Everything Gauge stores — vehicle entries, pairing keys, logs, settings and purchase state — lives inside the app on your phone. Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is nothing held anywhere else to delete.

Children

Gauge is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Since Gauge collects nothing, changes are expected to be rare.
